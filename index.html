Training principleSlow down.
Own the movement.
Progression

Build control first.
Then build capacity.

Keep the structure familiar while gradually improving the quality and challenge of each movement.

01
Weeks 1–2

Master form and slow tempo.

Learn each movement with a controlled 3-second lowering phase. Prioritize position, balance and confidence.

02
Weeks 3–4

Gradually increase reps.

Move toward the upper end of each prescribed range while keeping the same clean technique.

03
Weeks 5–6

Extend the pauses.

Where prescribed, gradually extend the bottom pause from 1 second toward 2–3 seconds without losing control.

Progress should feel sustainable.

Consistency and movement quality matter more than rushing to make a session harder.

Key rule

Movement control.

Lower slowly with a 3-second count and pause at the point of maximum tension when the exercise calls for it.

Quality over speed.A controlled rep is the goal—not simply completing the movement quickly.
PCOS tip

Keep intensity steady.

Focus on sustainable training and consistent movement rather than chasing exhaustion. Individual responses to exercise vary.
